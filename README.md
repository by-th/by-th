I lead GTM at [Kizzy](https://kizzy.io), where I'm co-founder and CEO, and engineered most of our growth stack myself. More about me at [t-h.xyz](https://t-h.xyz).

Some of what I've built:

- **Real-time promotions and offers, optimized around LTV and UA.** Every [promo](https://promo.kizzy.io), welcome offer and VIP deal runs from one registry and ships in 12 languages. Offers size themselves to the player: a [new player's](https://promo.kizzy.io/newplayer) biggest qualifying bet sets their free-bet tier, and [VIPs build their own offer](https://promo.kizzy.io/vip) and get paid as they play. Rewards land the moment a player qualifies, with no one on the team touching them.
- **24/7 player support agent.** Every ticket from Discord, email and the app goes to an agent first. It answers in the player's language across 12 languages, facilitates verification, and then pulls their deposits, bets and bonuses to give a real answer.
- **Player risk engine.** Every account is checked at sign-up, before any promotional offer, and again after their first deposit: shared wallets, IPs and funders, sign-up bursts, opposite-side bets, farm domains. This ensures that promotions and ads have higher efficiency and go to real players.
- **Lifecycle email system.** Covers the whole player journey automatically, from registration to offer sharing and retention. Across 34,422 sends, account-update emails opened at 41.4% with 8.5% clicks, against 22.1% and 0.7% for promotions.
- **Ads optimizer.** Agents build the ads and a landing page for each one in the player's language. The optimizer reprices every ad spot every 30 minutes on cost per real click and cuts the spots that don't convert.
- **[brew.md](https://brew.md).** A study page with a clock, a timer and ambient sound ([source](https://github.com/by-th/brew-md)).
