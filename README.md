I lead GTM at [Kizzy](https://kizzy.io), where I'm co-founder and CEO. Most of what I ship day to day is our GTM stack: custom promos and offer pages in 12 languages, our entire email lifecycle, and end-to-end customer data tracking. I build nearly all of it with Claude Code.

I built our paid acquisition loop. Agents make the ads and the landing page behind each one, in the player's language. An optimizer reprices every ad spot every 30 minutes on cost per real click and cuts the spots that don't convert, and every correction goes back into the agents, so each round of creative starts from the last.

I automated player support end to end. Every ticket goes to an agent first. It answers in the player's language across all 12 the app supports, proves who they are in code, pulls their deposits, bets and bonuses, and replies in seconds. A person only sees money moves, security and legal questions. Bonuses pay out the same way: when a player qualifies, the free bet or spins land without anyone touching it.

I obsess over the player experience: how fast a deposit lands, whether an offer makes sense on first read, and how quickly support gets someone a real answer.
